Categories, fixed items and budgets

Categories and rules

Categories are grouped by theme, and each transaction is filed under a category. Rules assign categories automatically: a rule matches the label, the merchant or the provider category, either with a contains test or a regular expression. Rules run at import time or on demand.

Fixed items

Recurring expenses and income (monthly, quarterly or yearly). Incoming transactions are matched against them automatically, and the app lists what is upcoming and what is overdue.

Budgets

Set a cap per category. Unused or overspent balance can be carried over to the next month. Centime also projects your spending at the current pace and keeps a history.

Month plan

Fixed items and budgets on a single page. The remainder of the month is: fixed income − fixed expenses − envelopes.

Dashboard

Bank balance, spending, income and net for the month compared with the previous one, points of attention, 12-month income and spending, spending by category, balance trend, budgets, due dates and the latest transactions.
